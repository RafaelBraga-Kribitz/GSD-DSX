<objective>
Build the decision-record substrate: schema, append-only crash-safe emitter, tolerant reader, and
the per-invocation header that groups one gate run's trail (REQ-P6-07, D-13 through D-19).

Purpose: this is the first write path in a codebase that until now only reads. Every later
v2.0.0 family emits records at its key judgment points; `dsx explain` (plan 06-09) renders them.
Getting the durability and the tolerant-read halves right here is what makes the trail
trustworthy — fsync makes the completed line survive a crash, tolerant reading makes the
half-written one harmless.

Output: `dsx/decisions.py` and `tests/test_decisions.py`. No CLI wiring and no caller yet —
plan 06-06 populates records from the structural validators, plan 06-09 writes them from the gate.
