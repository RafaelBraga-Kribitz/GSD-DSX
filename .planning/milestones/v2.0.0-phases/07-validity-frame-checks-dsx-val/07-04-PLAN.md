<objective>
Ship the two unit checks, and repair the two files those checks would otherwise break in the same
commit.

Purpose: `DSX-VAL-020` is the check the phase goal names most directly — an analysis run at a
finer grain than assignment guarantees pseudo-replication, and the finding has to make the
consequence legible rather than merely name it. `DSX-VAL-021` is the drift axis that makes the
disjointness requirement achievable by splitting axes instead of by suppression logic.

Two files trip `DSX-VAL-020` exactly as they stand today: the template, whose three unit
placeholders are three different strings, and the shared-budget interference fixture, which
declares an impression-level observation against a user-level assignment with no method family.
The research plan recommended landing fixture repairs in a late wave. That recommendation is
wrong here and this plan departs from it deliberately: a repair that lands after the check leaves
the suite red between waves, and two of the regression assertions that must stay green are among
the casualties. Each repair lands with the check that would otherwise break it.

Output: two new private helpers in the check module, a repaired template, a repaired corpus
fixture, and a regression test proving the two unit checks cannot collide.
</objective>
