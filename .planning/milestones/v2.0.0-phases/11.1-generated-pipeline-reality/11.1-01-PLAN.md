<objective>
Widen the entrypoint scan so that cleaning statistics computed over the full frame are caught by
reading the pandas idioms cleaning is actually written in, and so that a fit call placed after an
honest split is checked against what it was actually fitted on.

Purpose: today the scan only asks whether a fit-shaped call precedes a split marker. The
2026-08-20 reproduction never writes a fit call during cleaning — it imputes with a full-frame mean
inside a fillna call — and it places its scaler call after a real `train_test_split(...)` while
still passing the full frame. Both idioms pass the current scan with zero findings.

Output: two new finding codes in `dsx/checks/code.py`, a locked training-frame name lexicon,
tests, decision records, and a current finding catalogue.
</objective>
