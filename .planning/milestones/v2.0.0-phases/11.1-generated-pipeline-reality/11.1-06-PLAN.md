<objective>
Make a reported score say where it came from, and make a margin smaller than the model's own
fold-to-fold variation say so out loud.

Purpose: the baseline comparison is the single number most readouts turn on, and today the contract
has no field for its provenance. A cross-validation mean and a best-fold score are different claims
about the same model, and the second one is a selection artefact. A margin of one point over the
baseline means nothing when the model's own folds vary by three.

Output: two new optional results fields, two new finding codes, template scaffold, tests, a decision
record and a current catalogue.
</objective>

## Flagged assumptions

- The edge probe classified REQ-P11.1-05 as unclassified, so no edge predicate was derived for it
  mechanically. Three boundary cases the requirement does not settle are decided here and authored
  into `must_haves.truths` above: a margin exactly equal to the fold spread does not fire, on the
  reading that "below the fold spread" is strictly below; fewer than two parseable fold scores
  produce no spread and therefore no advisory; and an unrecognised score source is treated as a
  blank one. All three are assumptions, not probe results.
- The requirement calls the fold-spread finding advisory without naming a severity, and no discuss
  pass ran. This plan locks it at MEDIUM, on the severity ladder's own words: MEDIUM is a real
  defect that should be stated as a limitation if not fixed, and LOW is presentation and hygiene. A
  margin inside the model's own variation is the former.

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| specification author to gate | The score source and the fold-score list are analyst-supplied and untrusted. A non-numeric entry must be skipped, not raised on. |
| declared numbers to derived numbers | The spread is computed by the gate from declared values. Any richer computation would move the gate from adjudicating declarations to producing statistics, which this milestone's own boundary forbids. |
| repository fixtures to regression suite | The good spec, the bad spec and the template all carry a results block these branches now read. |
