<objective>
Build the fallback-rule mini-language and the closed fact registry it reads, as pure logic with no
finding emission and no gate registration.

no parser of any kind exists in this codebase today. Isolating it here keeps it fully unit-testable
and leaves every existing guard green, because none of the five D-13 guards trip until the first
`report.add("DSX-PRE-...")` call site exists (verified: `dsx/suppressions.py::known_codes()` AST-scans
`dsx/**/*.py` for literal `report.add` codes, independent of registration).

Output: `PREREG_FACTS` in `dsx/spec.py`, the parser and resolver in a new `dsx/frame/prereg.py`, and
the first three test classes in a new `tests/test_frame_prereg.py`.
