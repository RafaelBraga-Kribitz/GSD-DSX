### Planner decision — exclusions shape (RESEARCH Open Question 1)

The `exclusions` sub-block is accepted as EITHER a single rule-dict OR a list of rule-dicts, each
entry carrying `{rule, action, applied_before_split, justification}`. Both checks normalise to a
list of entries and iterate. This dissolves the dict-vs-list ambiguity (either shape satisfies
every locked decision and the D-15(d) fixtures) and is cheap to narrow later if desired.
