<objective>
Wire the adjudicator into the gate and into `dsx recommend-test`, and prove the whole committed
corpus is unmoved except where it was designed to move.

Purpose: this is where the check becomes real. Registration decides where the family runs at all;
severity, already fixed in plan 11-06, decides what blocks once it does. The frequentist-only
routing is computed outside the adjudicator, because making the module decide for itself would
require it to read the declared inferential school, which the boundary scanner forbids, and making
it paradigm-independent would make an honest Bayesian declaration more expensive than silence.

Output: `CHECKS["admissibility"]`, three `GATE_PROFILES` entries, a `run_checks` dispatch branch,
`--spec` and `--phase-dir` on `dsx recommend-test`, an additive `admissibility` key in its JSON
output, and a durable corpus regression test.
</objective>
