<objective>
Require a usable re-visit trigger on any recommendation or experiment — a named metric with a
threshold AND a time anchor — and block at plan when it is missing or vague. "When the market changes"
is rejected; "activation_rate below +1.0pp at the 2026-Q4 review" is accepted.

Purpose: a recommendation with no declared condition for revisiting it is a permanent commitment made
before the data. D-07 adds decision.revisit_when, required for a prescriptive question OR an experiment,
validated by a NEW sibling predicate — reusing the falsifier core (metric + threshold) and conjoining a
window clause, WITHOUT mutating falsifier_is_discriminating(), because that shared function's estimand
callers validate a windowless falsifier and the good fixture would otherwise newly fail DSX-VAL-011.

Output: the sibling predicate + a bounded window regex, _check_revisit_completeness firing DSX-COH-040
CRITICAL at plan, the catalogue allowlist entry, and every experiment fixture prophylaxed.
</objective>
