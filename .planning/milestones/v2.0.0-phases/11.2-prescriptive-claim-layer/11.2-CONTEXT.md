# Phase 11.2: Prescriptive claim layer - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-08-24 (assumptions mode + expert-persona round — Architect / Statistician / Auditor)
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

The claim register can express an **action recommendation** (`prescriptive`), and the gate then
holds it to the standard of the causal claim it is — including what would retire it
(`decision.revisit_when`) and how many times the locked plan was amended after results existed
(a spec-identity amendment counter). Plus two honesty-surface fixes: `dsx explain` renders what
the gate took on trust, and the storyteller is repointed at fields a gate actually reads.

**In scope:** `prescriptive` as a claim type at coherence-ladder rank 4; extending the existing
identification-strength enforcement to prescriptive claims; widening the causal-verb lexicon so
bare-infinitive/gerund action forms are caught; a required `decision.revisit_when` field with a
window-aware falsifier; a top-level `spec_id` plus an amendment counter over the Phase-6 decision
trail; a self-reported-fields view in `dsx explain`; quarantining reader-less scaffold fields and
repointing the storyteller.

**Out of scope, and not to be widened during planning:**
- A general recommendation/imperative lexicon (`offer`, `recommend`, `should`, `prioritise`,
  `allocate`) — a *new capability*, deferred (see D-15). Phase 11.2 catches recommendations that
  carry a causal verb ("...to **reduce** churn"), not verbless recommendations.
- Computing any test statistic or posterior on the gate path (brief D-02).
- Making a frame-layer (`dsx/frame/*`) check read `inference.paradigm` or import `dsx.checks`
  (brief D-11, `tests/test_frame_boundary.py`).
- A tamper-proof / signed decision trail — the amendment counter is a *committed-trail* honesty
  signal, not a security control (see D-12).
- Bayesian anything (gated backlog, brief §6.5).
</domain>

  reversing a decision needs a reversal record.

  shipped vocabulary rather than add a parallel one. M-02 (`DSX-PAR-010/011` read the existing
  `design.peeking_policy`, no new `stopping_rule` field) and M-09 (`method_family_required` reuses

  ladder row: a prescriptive claim is silently skipped at `coherence.py:61-62` (`if ctype not in
  CLAIM_STRENGTH: continue`). *(Architect + Auditor, unanimous; one-fact-one-code + D-06.)*

- **D-02: ORDERING CONSTRAINT / hard precondition for REQ-01 and REQ-02.** The `CLAIM_TYPES` +
  `CLAIM_STRENGTH` edit **and** the two stale "four claim types" remedy strings at `spec.py:840`
  (`DSX-SPEC-061`) **and** `spec.py:849` (`DSX-SPEC-062`) must land in the same plan slice that

  current corpus: **no committed fixture declares a `type: prescriptive` claim** (only
  `question_type: prescriptive`), so adding the member changes no existing verdict.

### Prescriptive claims and identification strength (REQ-P11.2-02)

- **D-03: extend the existing causal identification codes `DSX-CLM-020` / `DSX-CLM-021` to
  prescriptive claims by widening the `ctype` gate at `claims.py:147`; mint no new code; amend the
  requirement's flat "HIGH" to causal-parity severity — blank/none → CRITICAL (`DSX-CLM-020`),
  weak → HIGH (`DSX-CLM-021`).** The finding message is parameterised on claim type so a
  prescriptive claim reads "prescriptive claim recommends an intervention on <none/weak>
  identification" rather than the causal wording. *(Statistician + Auditor decisive-lane, over the
  Architect's new-code proposal.)*

  **PERSONA DECISION (loud, §4; rigour > reliability > flexibility) — this AMENDS REQ-P11.2-02;
  the human may veto via the daily summary before the phase ships.** Two things change from the
  literal requirement:
  1. **Severity: flat HIGH → CRITICAL for blank/none, HIGH for weak.** A prescriptive claim is
     decision-theoretically *stronger* than a causal one (it asserts an effect **and** that acting
     on it is warranted — Manski's treatment-choice framing). A flat HIGH would create a perverse
     dodge: relabelling a no-identification claim `causal → prescriptive` moves it from
     `DSX-CLM-020` CRITICAL to a softer HIGH — making the more ambitious claim *cheaper*, the exact
     inversion brief D-10 exists to block. Parity with the tightest sibling closes it.
  2. **New check → reuse.** Reusing the identical codes for causal and prescriptive means
     relabelling buys **zero** severity arbitrage — the dodge is closed structurally, not by
     policy. It preserves the none/weak split the Auditor requires (separately suppressible), and
     — because no committed spec declares a prescriptive claim — it changes **no** existing verdict
     (the 11-CONTEXT D-03 suppression-invalidation hazard does not bite here). Consistent with the
     M-02/M-09 reuse doctrine.

  The check reads the **claim-layer** identification (`claim.get("identification") or strategy`,
  the source `_check_causal_support` already resolves at `claims.py:149,166`), **not**
  `validity_frame.identification.strength` — a frame-layer check cannot condition on claim type
  (D-11). The frame-layer `DSX-VAL-040` (CRITICAL) already gives the plan-time hard stop for a
  prescriptive *question* with no identification; `DSX-CLM-020/021` are the verify/ship *claim*-level
  backstop. **No hedge exemption:** unlike `DSX-CLM-021`, a prescriptive claim's hedging does not
  rescue it — a recommendation is an action commitment, not a probabilistic statement.

### Causal-verb lexicon: widening, tiers, provenance (REQ-P11.2-03)

- **D-04: widen the lexicon and reinterpret "two tiers" as a false-positive-conservative context
  gate, NOT an epistemic softener; mint no code (routes to existing `DSX-CLM-010/011` +
  `DSX-COH-010`).** `CAUSAL_VERBS` (`spec.py:52-60`) today has `increases/decreases/reduces/
  reduced/boosts/lifts/causing/driving` but **lacks** the bare infinitives `reduce/increase/
  decrease` and gerunds `reducing/increasing`, so the flagship "offer bundled incentives **to
  reduce** churn" evades detection entirely. Widen it to catch those forms.

  **PERSONA DECISION (loud, §4) — this AMENDS REQ-P11.2-03's "gerunds via the hedging path".** The
  Statistician (backed by the phase's own Hernán 2018 anchor) established that **a gerund is not a
  hedge** — "increasing spend drives revenue" is a flat causal assertion in nominalised form, and
  routing it to the softer `DSX-CLM-010` MEDIUM path would be a category error that double-counts
  against the existing text-level `HEDGE_TERMS` split (`claims.py:30-34`). The correct two tiers:
  - **Tier 1 — unambiguous action/causal forms** (imperatives, bare infinitives in a
    recommendation/purpose construction, and the existing finite causal verbs) → full strength
    (`DSX-CLM-011` / `DSX-COH-010` CRITICAL).
  - **Tier 2 — ambiguous forms** (gerunds; noun-homograph bare forms like "increase"/"decrease"
    that are also nouns) → routed through a **conservative purpose/recommendation-context gate**
    ("**to** reduce churn", "in order to increase"), **not** through epistemic softening.
  - **The `HEDGE_TERMS` epistemic gate remains the sole softener** — only genuine hedges ("may",
    "associated with") route to MEDIUM. "reducing churn *may* be possible" → both verb and hedge →
    MEDIUM; "increasing spend drives revenue" → verb, no hedge → CRITICAL.

- **D-05: causal-verb provenance is an umbrella structural-criterion citation + an explicit
  editorial-closure header, NOT per-member citations.** A verb lexicon has no per-member published
  "reference value" — attaching a paper to `boosts` would be citation theatre. Anchor the *why*
  (surface form is flagged regardless of morphology) to **Hernán 2018 "The C-Word"** as a
  `Structural criterion:` (D-05's structural branch), and declare closure as editorial judgement via
  a `vocabulary_is_not_exhaustive: true`-style header — the exact model 11-CONTEXT D-10 established
  for the assumption vocabulary ("no published closed lexicon exists; closure is this project's
  editorial judgement and the file must say so"). Optional companion for the *classification* (which
  verbs are causal/action): Levin 1993 lexical causatives, as a structural criterion, not per member
  — human decides at ship (D-16). *(Statistician.)*

- **D-06: the widening ships with a mandatory false-positive regression guard.** *(Auditor T-4 +
  Statistician DEC-3c.)* The plan MUST include: (a) a **golden-file finding-set-equality** test on
  `examples/good-ANALYSIS-SPEC.yaml` (and every `examples/**` fixture) asserting the finding set is
  **unchanged** by the widening; (b) a **negative fixture** — an `association`/`descriptive` claim
  containing "gain"/"sales increase in Q4" asserting **zero** new findings; (c) the full known-bad
  corpus re-run green. The live collision sites — `weak-identification-mmm` ("associated with an
  increase in revenue", typed `causal`) and `good` ("the activation increase", an interpretation
  field) — are exempt today (`_check_causal_language` early-returns for `ctype=="causal"`,
  `claims.py:111-112`; `_check_decision_language` only runs for descriptive/diagnostic,
  `coherence.py:84`), and must stay exempt. Prefer `\b`-bounded matching and purpose-gating so
  "sales increase in Q4" (a noun) does not trip; `_INCIDENTAL_GAP_CODES`
  (`tests/test_known_bad_corpus.py:64-80`) stays unchanged.

### `decision.revisit_when` and the window-aware falsifier (REQ-P11.2-04)

- **D-07: a new `decision.revisit_when` field, required for `question_type: prescriptive` OR
  `design.kind: experiment`, validated by a NEW SIBLING predicate — not by mutating the shared
  falsifier — firing new code `DSX-COH-040` CRITICAL.** *(Architect + Statistician.)*
  - **Requiredness lives in `dsx/checks/coherence.py`** (a new `_check_revisit_completeness`),
    because `coherence.check` already threads `qtype` (`:47-51`) and `_check_experiment_decision`
    already reads `design.kind` (`:111-143`). It is decision *completeness*, not decision *shape*,
    so it does not belong in `spec.py::_validate_decision`, and it is a distinct concept from
    experiment-MPE completeness (the `DSX-COH-020` decade) → **new decade 040**.
  - **One code, both triggers** (prescriptive and experiment are two triggers of one fact — "no
    usable re-visit trigger declared"); blank / `<placeholder>` / refusal all collapse into it.
  - **Severity CRITICAL, plan-blocking** — `coherence` is in the plan profile (`cli.py:113-129`),
    consistent with `DSX-COH-020`'s CRITICAL; `revisit_when` is a pre-commitment made before data.
  - **Predicate: `revisit_when_is_discriminating(x) = falsifier_is_discriminating(x) AND
    has_window_token(x)`.** Do **not** extend `falsifier_is_discriminating()` (`spec.py:491-501`)
    itself — its two estimand callers (`DSX-VAL-011` at `val.py:236`, and `val.py:637`) validate a
    *logical* falsifier with no time anchor, and the `good` fixture's estimand falsifier has no
    window (`good:303`); adding a window requirement to the shared predicate would newly fire
    `DSX-VAL-011` on the good fixture — a shipped-firing-set regression that violates this phase's
    corpus-green constraint. Reuse the falsifier *core* (metric + threshold) and conjoin a window
    clause. **Valid window** = a duration (`30 days`, `8 weeks`, `next quarter`), a date/deadline
    (ISO date, `by 2026-Q4`), or a time-anchored recurring event (`at the next quarterly review`).
    **Invalid** = open-ended state-change with no time anchor ("when the market changes",
    "as needed") — the `revisit_when` analogue of a non-discriminating falsifier.

### Spec identity, the amendment counter, and its limits (REQ-P11.2-05)

- **D-08: identity is a new top-level operator-declared `spec_id`, REQUIRED for
  prescriptive/experiment specs and FAIL-CLOSED (absent at verify/ship → `DSX-PRE-040` HIGH); it
  MUST be top-level, outside `validity_frame`/`inference`, so it never pollutes `frame_digest`.**
  *(Architect + Auditor decisive-lane.)* Derived identities were rejected: `title+question_type`
  is perturbable by a one-char title edit → counter resets, and collides genuinely-different specs
  on a shared root (re-importing the Phase-10 cross-spec FP); `decision_rule+owner` is
  self-defeating because the `decision_rule` change *is* the amendment we want to catch. An explicit
  declared id is stable by construction and, being top-level, is automatically excluded from
  `frame_digest`'s `{validity_frame, inference}` inputs — so it never becomes the thing it counts.
  Fail-closed is essential: an optional id could be dodged by omission.

- **D-09: the amendment signal = distinct `frame_digest` values under one `spec_id`, PLUS an
  identity-free whole-trail-root floor, scoped to where `DSX-PRE-020` is silent.** *(Auditor
  decisive-lane.)* Fire `DSX-PRE-041` HIGH at verify/ship when >1 distinct `frame_digest` appears
  under one `spec_id` with no clearing amendment entry. **Add an identity-agnostic floor:** also
  count distinct `frame_digest` over the *whole* committed trail root (Phase-10 membership style) —
  renaming `spec_id` after a bad result does not erase the earlier header's digest from the same
  file, so the floor is the un-renameable backstop and costs no new record shape. **Scope:** the
  counter is *redundant* with `DSX-PRE-020` (CRITICAL, harder) for `declared_at: pre_data` specs;
  its only non-redundant coverage is `post_data`/blank `declared_at` — "frame-shopping under
  honest-post-hoc cover", the case `DSX-PRE-020` deliberately leaves silent. The plan scopes the
  counter there and records why (D-12).

- **D-10: the amendment entry is a new `record_type: "amendment"` in `DECISIONS.jsonl`, and its
  clearing bar is `is_placeholder_or_refusal` + a two-digest reference — NOT the falsifier
  predicate.** *(Architect storage + Auditor T-3 clearing bar.)* Storage is the append-only,
  crash-durable, content-locked trail — not a mutable `amendments:` spec block that can drift from
  what happened. The entry records **when** (`invocation_id, gate_point, dsx_version`), **which**
  (`spec_id`), **what changed** (`prev_frame_digest`, `new_frame_digest` — the two it reconciles),
  and **why** (`reason`, operator-declared); `spec_id` is added to `InvocationHeader`. The `reason`
  must pass `is_placeholder_or_refusal` (`spec.py:476-488`) — "updated analysis" is rejected — **and**
  must reference the two `frame_digest`s it reconciles (both machine-verifiable as existing headers),
  so the clearing entry is *verifiable*, not merely *present*. Do **not** reuse
  `falsifier_is_discriminating()` on the prose `reason`: its numeric/comparison requirement is a
  category error for a rationale and would false-reject an honest "narrowed the segment to non-bot
  signups". A brittle phrase stoplist ("updated analysis") is also rejected — defeated by rephrasing.

- **D-11: `DSX-PRE-040` (missing `spec_id` when required) and `DSX-PRE-041` (uncleared amendment)
  both ship HIGH in the `prereg` check, verify/ship profiles only.** Reuse the `DSX-PRE-*` family
  (already in `_D05_ALLOWLIST_PREFIXES`, thematically pre-registration integrity) over a new
  `DSX-AMD-*` prefix (which would need a `PREFIX_GROUPS` row + an allowlist entry). New decade 040 —
  the amendment ledger is a distinct concept from the `DSX-PRE-020` content lock. HIGH is correct:
  the signal cannot exist at plan (it needs results) and it is clearable, so it is not
  plan-blocking CRITICAL. *(Architect + Statistician severity.)*

- **D-12: RESIDUAL LIMITS — record, do not paper over.** *(Auditor.)* The amendment counter is a
  **committed-trail honesty signal, not a tamper-proof control.** These are stated in the finding
  remedy and in the README known-limits, the way `DSX-PRE-020` states its own:
  - **Local-file reset/tamper.** `DECISIONS.jsonl` is a plain file the gate writes; `rm`/truncation
    resets `next_invocation_id` to `INV-0001` (`decisions.py:182-184`); `read_all` is *tolerant*
    (undecodable bytes `errors="replace"`, unparseable lines skipped — `decisions.py:149,159-160`),
    so selective line-deletion is invisible; `frame_digest` is **unsigned** change-detection
    (`decisions.py:190`), so an older header's digest can be hand-edited to collapse the count. The
    counter means something only over a VCS-tracked, review-visible trail.
  - **Blind to everything outside `{validity_frame, inference}`.** A same-frame / swapped-recommendation,
    swapped-claim-type, or retitled amendment never moves `frame_digest`. A clean count is not
    evidence the *conclusion* was stable — that is T-1/coherence territory (D-15).
  - **Pre_data redundancy + re-opened cross-spec FP.** Recorded so the HIGH is understood as covering
    the post-hoc gap, and so the identity floor's re-import of the Phase-10 cross-spec FP risk is
    disclosed.
  - **The `why` is checkable for form, not truth.** Even the two-digest bar confirms *which* blocks
    changed, never whether the stated reason is real. Consistent with the standing limit "a frame
    that lies passes."

### `dsx explain` self-reported view (REQ-P11.2-06)

- **D-13: render a separately-labelled "self-reported (taken on trust, not verified by dsx)"
  section AFTER the decision trail in `_render_decision_trail` (`cli.py:621-650`); mint no code;
  leave `escalate` untouched and preserve the returns-0 pure-reader contract.** *(Architect.)* The
  trusted set = everything the gate *compares but never computes*: all `validity_frame.*`,
  `inference.*`, `analysis.test`, `declared_at`. A separate labelled section (not interleaving into
  the decision records) is the clearest trust-boundary disclosure and removes any risk of a reader
  mistaking a self-reported value for a computed verdict. **`frame_digest` stays in the computed
  column** (already in the header line) and must never be labelled self-reported — labelling a
  computed value "taken on trust" is the honesty inversion this project exists to prevent.
  `cmd_explain` loads the spec via the existing loader inside its `try/except` (`cli.py:583-618`),
  preserving return-0-by-construction; `escalate` (`decisions.py:83`, still unrendered) is not
  touched. The exact field set is a bounded plan-time detail within the "compared-but-never-computed,
  never a computed value" constraint (Claude's Discretion).

### Scaffold-field quarantine and storyteller repoint (REQ-P11.2-07)

- **D-14: move reader-less scaffold fields to a clearly-commented "documentation-only — NOT
  gate-read" block in `templates/ANALYSIS-SPEC.yaml`; do NOT delete; repoint the storyteller; mint
  no code.** *(Architect.)* `decision.reversible` (`:37`) and `decision.deadline` (`:38`) are read

  removal loses their human-documentation value; the real hazard is the *false impression of

  "not gate-read" label cures directly. The template comments must **distinguish** the newly-enforced

  operators will conflate two temporal decision fields. Repoint `agents/dsx-data-storyteller.md`
  (`:20-28,53`) and its sibling `skills/dsx-narrate/SKILL.md` at only gate-read fields; update the
  template claim-type comment (`:273`) from four types to five. A full audit of reader-less fields
  beyond `reversible`/`deadline` is a plan-time task (Claude's Discretion).

### The residual recommendation-without-a-verb hole (records a known limit)

- **D-15: a `descriptive`-typed claim phrased as a recommendation with NO causal verb ("offer
  bundled incentives", "the optimal action is X", "prioritise segment Y") is caught by neither the
  identification check (D-03) nor the causal-verb widening (D-04) — this is a NAMED LIMIT, and
  closing it (a recommendation/imperative lexicon) is DEFERRED to a future phase.** *(Auditor T-1
  residual.)* The flagship "offer bundled incentives **to reduce** churn" *is* caught because it
  carries "reduce". Adding an imperative lexicon (`offer/recommend/should/prioritise/allocate`) is
  a new capability beyond the seven requirements — the scope guardrail sends it to Deferred Ideas.
  The limit must be stated in CONTEXT/README, not left implied.
