<objective>
Give the decision trail a spec identity and count how many times the locked plan was amended after
results existed. A prescriptive/experiment spec must declare a top-level spec_id (fail-closed); more
than one distinct frame_digest under that identity, uncleared, fires HIGH at verify/ship.

Purpose: today the content lock (DSX-PRE-020) clears with no finding on a post_data/blank declared_at
spec whose frame was shopped after results — Phase 10 deliberately left InvocationHeader with no spec
identity (prereg.py:532-538) to avoid cross-spec false positives on a shared root. REQ-05 reintroduces
an identity and must re-solve that risk: a declared, top-level, fail-closed spec_id, plus an
identity-free whole-trail floor as the un-renameable backstop. It is a committed-trail honesty signal,
not a tamper-proof control — the residual limits are recorded, not papered over (D-12).

Output: spec_id on the header, an AmendmentRecord shape, DSX-PRE-040/041 in the prereg check at
verify/ship, the writer wired, and every experiment fixture prophylaxed with a spec_id.
</objective>
