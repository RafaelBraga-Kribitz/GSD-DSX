# Retention-bundle rollout readout

**Audience:** VP Customer Success
**Decision:** roll the retention bundle out to all at-risk accounts if the
n-weighted retention uplift clears the +1.0pp floor.

## Headline

Roll the retention bundle out to all at-risk accounts: it raises 90-day
retention by 3.2 percentage points on the n-weighted aggregate (95% CI 2.4
to 4.0pp) across the at-risk book in the test window.

The interval clears the pre-declared +1.0pp practical floor across the full
book of 20,000 accounts (n=20,000; 10,000 per arm), so the decision rule
recommends rollout. No guardrail degraded beyond its declared tolerance over
the two-week window.

## Segments

The uplift is not uniform across the book:

- A (high-value, n=4,000): +5.0pp
- B (mid, n=3,000): +4.0pp
- C (established, n=2,000): +3.0pp
- D (minority, n=1,000): -6.0pp

## Limitations

This estimates the effect for at-risk accounts only and says nothing about the
healthy base. It was measured over two weeks; seasonal cohorts may respond
differently.
