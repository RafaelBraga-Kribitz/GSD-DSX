<objective>
Grow `dsx/profiler.py::profile_csv` to compute, stdlib-only and hermetically, the per-column
numeric and categorical trust-core numbers the EDA explore protocol currently asks the agent to
eyeball, additively and with every pre-existing key byte-stable (D-01 nesting; D-02 statistical
definitions). This is the first change to the profiler dict and it establishes the byte-stability
guard the rest of the phase depends on.

Purpose: `EDA.md` copies these numbers instead of re-deriving them, and two runs carry identical
numbers by construction (REQ-P25-01, REQ-P25-02).
Output: `columns[<col>].numeric` and `columns[<col>].categorical` sub-maps; a new hermetic test
module with hand-computed reference-value fixtures; a determinism guard and a pre-existing-key
golden.

D-01 (additive nesting), D-02 (statistical definitions, inclusive/type-7 quantiles, sample sd,
null-not-zero), and D-04 (guard shape — determinism + pre-existing-key golden) are the binding
decisions this plan implements.
</objective>
