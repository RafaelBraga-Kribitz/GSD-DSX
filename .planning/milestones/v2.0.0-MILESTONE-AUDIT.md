## 2. The one scope-partial — REQ-P7-08 (adjudicated by §4 advisor round)

REQ-P7-08 ("A measurement construct with no operationalisation, **or** whose known gaps
contradict the claim population, is blocked") is two clauses joined by "or". Clause 1 is MET
and tested (`DSX-VAL-070`). Clause 2 is deliberately unadjudicated under decision D-06
(07-CONTEXT.md, 2026-08-10, pre-implementation): `known_gaps` is read by no measurement check.

The mechanical status matrix maps (VERIFICATION passed + SUMMARY listed + REQUIREMENTS `[ ]`)
to "satisfied — update checkbox". **The audit deliberately declines that mechanical move.**
Both advisor personas (Architect + adversarial Auditor, opus) independently concluded that
flipping the checkbox to `[x]` would be the dodge — it would falsely assert clause 2 is
implemented — while classifying REQ-P7-08 as **satisfied-within-declared-scope** is the honest
verdict:

- The **binding** Phase-7 acceptance criterion (ROADMAP SC4) requires only clause 1, which is
  met and tested. The requirement *text* carries the aspirational second clause; the SC is the
  operative gate.
- Clause 2's deferral is a **pre-registered scope decision** (D-06), not a shortfall discovered
  at audit, and it is **architecturally forced**: any text-comparison rule strong enough to
  catch it fails the good fixture under D-08, and D-02 forbids heavier gate-path computation.
  Its documented entry condition is the Phase 12 calibration corpus.
- The precedence rule invoked (**D-09**: where REQUIREMENTS text and ROADMAP SC diverge, the SC
  binds) is not minted for this occasion — it was already applied to REQ-P7-03 earlier in the
  same phase. Consistency, not opportunism.
- The milestone already **discloses up front** (REQ-P6-15) that "a frame which lies passes";
  clause 2 is exactly a semantic-lie case the milestone never promised to catch.

**Decision (loud, §4, vetoable via the daily summary):** REQ-P7-08 = satisfied-within-declared-scope;
milestone = passed. Checkbox stays `[ ]`; traceability stays `Partial`. Follow-up (non-blocking):
a future milestone amends the REQ-P7-08 text to match delivered scope, or promotes clause 2 to a
backlog requirement gated on the Phase 12 corpus.
