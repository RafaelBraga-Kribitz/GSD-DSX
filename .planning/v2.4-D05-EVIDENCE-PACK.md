## What "confirmed-by-loop" means here (be precise)

The loop has **not** fetched these sources live this firing. The *confirmed-by-loop*
column records only: (a) what the 2026-08-29 scope research (`V2.3-V2.4-SCOPE.md`
§3) already recorded per source, plus (b) bibliographic facts the loop holds with
high confidence (author/year/venue/structure). The *UNVERIFIED-for-human* column is
the authenticity that only a primary-source read can settle: **that the exact claim

operator confirming that column at the source.

what we say it is" — not a per-entry deep read, or the burden triples to 90+.
