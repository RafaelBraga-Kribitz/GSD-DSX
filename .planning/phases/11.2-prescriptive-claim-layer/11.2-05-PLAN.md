<objective>
Hold a prescriptive claim to the same identification standard as a causal one — because a
recommendation asserts both that an effect exists and that acting on it is warranted. Blank or none
identification is CRITICAL; weak is HIGH; and unlike a causal claim, hedging does not rescue it.

Purpose: `_check_causal_support` (claims.py:144-186) today gates on `if ctype != "causal": return`, so
a prescriptive claim recommending an intervention on an association is never evaluated. D-03 widens the
gate to include prescriptive, reusing DSX-CLM-020/021 so relabelling causal→prescriptive buys zero
severity arbitrage (the dodge brief D-10 exists to block), and removes the weak-path hedge exemption
for prescriptive claims — a recommendation is an action commitment, not a probabilistic statement.

Output: the widened ctype gate, a claim-type-parameterised message, a prescriptive-only no-hedge
branch, and tests proving parity plus causal-unchanged.
</objective>
