<objective>
Stop the template from implying that fields no gate reads are enforced. Move decision.reversible and
decision.deadline into a clearly-labelled documentation-only block, distinguish them from the newly
gate-read decision.revisit_when, update the claim-type comment to five types, and repoint the
storyteller and narrate skill at gate-read fields.

Purpose: an operator who sets reversible: true believing dsx checks it is misled — no check reads it
(grep-confirmed). Deletion loses the human-documentation value; the honest fix is an explicit
"not gate-read" label. The template must also not let an operator conflate the re-open trigger
(revisit_when, now enforced) with the decide-by date (deadline, scaffold).

Output: a quarantine block in the template, a distinction comment, a five-type claim comment, a
repointed storyteller/narrate, and a grep invariant that no check reads the quarantined fields.
</objective>
