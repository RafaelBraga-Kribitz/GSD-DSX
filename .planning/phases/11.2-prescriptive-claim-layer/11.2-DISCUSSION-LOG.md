## Persona Round — decisions and the disagreements resolved

Three genuine disagreements were resolved by the orchestrator on the rigour tie-break:

1. **REQ-02 severity + new-code-vs-reuse (Architect vs Statistician vs Auditor).** Architect
   proposed a new `DSX-CLM-022` at flat HIGH. Statistician showed flat HIGH creates a
   causal→prescriptive severity-relabel *dodge* and argued for causal parity (none CRITICAL / weak
   HIGH). Auditor (decisive, code-count lane) showed a single bundled HIGH code *under-splits* two
   facts and recommended reusing `DSX-CLM-020/021` by widening the `ctype` gate. **Resolved (D-03):
   reuse `DSX-CLM-020/021`, parameterised message, causal parity — 0 new codes.** Reuse closes the
   dodge structurally (identical codes = zero arbitrage), preserves the split, and changes no
   existing verdict (no prescriptive fixtures exist). This AMENDS REQ-P11.2-02 (loud, vetoable).

2. **REQ-03 "gerunds via the hedging path" (Statistician vs the requirement wording).** Statistician
   (backed by the phase's Hernán 2018 anchor) established a gerund is *not* a hedge — routing gerunds
   to the softer MEDIUM path is a category error. **Resolved (D-04): widen the lexicon; Tier 2
   (ambiguous forms) routes through a conservative purpose/recommendation-context gate, NOT epistemic
   softening; `HEDGE_TERMS` stays the sole softener.** AMENDS REQ-P11.2-03's wording (loud, vetoable).

3. **REQ-05 spec-identity design (Architect vs Auditor decisive lane).** Auditor showed all derived
   identities are gameable and that Phase 10 *deliberately* omitted an identity to avoid cross-spec
   FPs. **Resolved (D-08/D-09/D-10): explicit top-level `spec_id`, FAIL-CLOSED, + an identity-free
   whole-trail-root floor as the un-renameable backstop; amendment entry in `DECISIONS.jsonl` cleared
   only by a placeholder-passing `reason` that references the two reconciled `frame_digest`s; scoped
   to `post_data` where `DSX-PRE-020` is silent; residual limits recorded (D-12).**

Convergent (no dispute): D-01 (reuse `DSX-COH-001`), D-07 (new-sibling window predicate — extending
the shared falsifier would regress `DSX-VAL-011` on the good fixture), D-11 (`DSX-PRE-040/041` HIGH),
D-13 (separate self-reported section, `frame_digest` stays computed), D-14 (quarantine not delete),
