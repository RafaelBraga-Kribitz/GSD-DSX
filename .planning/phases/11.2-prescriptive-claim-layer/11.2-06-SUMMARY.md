## Decisions Made
- Reused `falsifier_is_discriminating()` unchanged rather than widening it, per the plan's explicit prohibition and RESEARCH landmine (b) — verified with a byte-diff and a positive regression test.
- Window-token regex kept to two bounded-quantifier regexes (duration, date/quarter) plus a third branch that is pure substring matching (no regex at all) for digit-free recurring-checkpoint phrases like "at the next quarterly review" — this sidesteps any backtracking-surface question for that branch entirely.
- `DSX-COH-040`'s citation names Nosek et al. (2018) "The preregistration revolution" with an explicit unverified-locator flag, matching this phase's established D-16/HQ-3 convention (see plans 02 and 03's flagged_assumptions) rather than inventing a page/section locator.
