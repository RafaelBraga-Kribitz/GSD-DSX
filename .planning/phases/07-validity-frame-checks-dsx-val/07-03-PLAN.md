<objective>
Create `dsx/frame/val.py`, ship the two estimand checks, and land every piece of build plumbing
the validity-frame family needs — all of it, in the order the existing invariants force.

Purpose: this is the plan where the family becomes real. Three of its edits are load-bearing in a
way that is invisible from the code alone. Removing the validity-frame prefix from the
not-shipped map in `dsx/frame/paradigm.py` must happen in the same commit as the first finding

another asserts every applied prefix resolves to at least one — before the commit the first
assertion holds and the second fails, after it the reverse, and no ordering satisfies both. The
prefix-group entry in the catalogue generator is what stops the renderer dropping the entire
family with a green build. The allow-list entry is what makes the citation rule apply to this
family at all, because the list is an include list, not an exclude list.

the paradigm-read invariant test, and a regenerated finding catalogue.
