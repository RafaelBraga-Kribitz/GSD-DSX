<objective>
Deliver REQ-P15-04's shipping half: a declaration-only changing-denominator check in
dsx/checks/metrics.py. Add `_check_cohort_denominator_shift`, dispatched unconditionally from
`check()`, that mints DSX-MET-021 (HIGH) when a `results.cohort_comparisons[]` entry declares buckets
whose `sampling_rate` (or `treatment_share`) differ beyond the declared-or-0.10-default tolerance AND
`reweighted` is not literal `True`. Prove it is provably disjoint from the pre-existing DSX-MET-020
(the key trap #1), gate-path-pure, and HIGH not CRITICAL.

Purpose: Kohavi's Pitfall 4 — a metric pooled across subpopulations sampled at different rates with no
reweighting — is a non-experimental, descriptive/diagnostic BI defect, so it must run OUTSIDE the
causal-gated `dsx/frame/interference.py` (which returns empty unless `needs_causal_block(spec)`).
Homing it in the unconditional `metrics.check()` under the MET family (its closest semantic sibling
DSX-MET-020 in the 02x denominator band) is the D-03 family placement. It is HIGH because a
declaration evidences only that the bucket allocation SHIFTED, not that the pooled sign REVERSED
(that fuller Simpson case is the CRITICAL MET-030). The survivorship half is deliberately NOT minted
(D-05).

Output: `_check_cohort_denominator_shift` (DSX-MET-021) + tests/test_cohort_denominator.py proving
firing, the MET-020/MET-021 disjointness, silence on well-behaved cohort declarations, and gate-path
purity, carrying the `# D-05: DSX-MET-021` marker.
</objective>
