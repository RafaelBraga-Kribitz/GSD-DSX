<objective>
Steal DAAF's data-onboarding artifact (REQ-P14-02 / D-03): a portable `DATA-DICTIONARY.md` sits next
to `DATA-PROFILE.yaml` so later sessions do not re-guess grain and join keys. Two edits, both off the
gate path: (1) ship `templates/DATA-DICTIONARY.md`, mirroring the `templates/EDA.md`
write-then-ungated precedent; (2) add an authoring step to `skills/dsx-explore-data/SKILL.md` that
writes the dictionary right after `dsx profile`, copying the column roster verbatim from the profile
and authoring only the semantics the CSV cannot carry.

Purpose: `DATA-PROFILE.yaml` carries the deterministic facts (row count, dtypes, null rates, unique
counts); it cannot carry grain, join keys, per-column meaning, source table or PII. Those semantics
are exactly what a later session re-guesses, and re-guessing grain is how "active users" and
"activity" quietly diverge. The dictionary forces the analyst to author them once, next to the
profile, keyed to the same extract.

Output: `templates/DATA-DICTIONARY.md` (the template) and an authoring step in
`skills/dsx-explore-data/SKILL.md`.
</objective>
