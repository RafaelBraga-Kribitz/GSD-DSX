<objective>
Land the two foundational corrections every later plan in this phase depends on: the
`dsx/loader.py` `_NULL` fix (REQ-P6-01) and the whole `dsx/spec.py` vocabulary layer
(REQ-P6-05, REQ-P6-06).

Purpose: four frame fields declare the literal `none` as a legitimate value
(`dependence.structure`, `interference.risk`, `interference.mitigation`,
`identification.constraint_source`). Until `_NULL` stops treating `none` as a null token they
parse as `None` and the whole `validity_frame:` contract is unreadable. ROADMAP ordering
constraint 1 makes this strictly first.

Output: a loader that agrees with PyYAML on `none`; ten new closed vocabularies; the
`uncontrolled_continuous` `PEEKING_POLICIES` member; a registry-backed `describe_vocabulary()`
that stops discarding descriptions; and the tests that pin all of it.
</objective>
