blocks, and pin the round-trip (REQ-P6-02, REQ-P6-04, REQ-P6-12, D-07, D-08, D-12).

`design.kind: experiment`, so the moment those validators land it needs all ten sub-blocks or
`test_good_fixture_passes_every_gate` goes red. The data lands first, and it lands inert: nothing
reads these blocks yet, and `validate_structure()` does not reject unknown top-level keys, so the
suite stays green at every task boundary.

Output: extended fixtures, a fully scaffolded template, and the round-trip tests that prove the
new contract surface parses. No validator logic — that is plan 06-06.
