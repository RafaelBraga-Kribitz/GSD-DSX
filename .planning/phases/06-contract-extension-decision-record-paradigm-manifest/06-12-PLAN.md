<objective>
Correct a false, committed, falsifiable claim about what the known-bad corpus guarantees, and pin
the corrected claim with the gate-level assertions the module never had (REQ-P6-13, D-06).

Purpose: all three `examples/known-bad/*-ANALYSIS-SPEC.yaml` header comments and the interference
post-mortem assert that today's checks pass these fixtures at every gate and every severity
threshold. `06-VERIFICATION.md` and `06-REVIEW.md` both reproduced the opposite directly: every
fixture exits 1 at `dsx gate verify` and at `dsx gate ship`, on findings that have nothing to do
with the defect each fixture exists to encode — a missing narrative path, no reproducibility
entrypoint, an unresolved evidence pointer, an unwaived assumption, a warehouse metric with no SQL.
ROADMAP Success Criterion 5's literal text only requires passing `dsx validate` structurally, which
genuinely holds, so this is a documentation-accuracy defect rather than a Success-Criterion
failure — but it is false, it is in the tracked repository, and `tests/test_known_bad_corpus.py`
only ever runs `dsx validate`, so nothing in the suite catches it. Phases 7, 8, 9 and 11 are
invited by the post-mortems to build regression tests on "passes today, blocks once the new code
ships" as a baseline, and that baseline is not the one the corpus actually provides.

Output: header and post-mortem prose that states what is true, plus three new corpus tests that
make the corrected statement mechanically enforced.
</objective>
