<objective>
Add the closed `ESTIMAND_TYPES` vocabulary and the optional `validity_frame.estimand.type` field
that the admissibility adjudicator keys on, then populate that field on all nine committed specs.

Purpose: the adjudicator needs a machine-readable estimand axis. The one candidate that reuses an
existing shape (`analysis.outcome_type` plus `n_groups` plus `paired`) is unreachable from
`examples/known-bad/weak-identification-mmm-ANALYSIS-SPEC.yaml`, which has no `analysis:` block and
no `model:` block at all — so the `linear_regression` family that fixture justifies could never be
traced to it. `validity_frame.estimand` is one of the six always-required sub-blocks, so a field
added there reaches every spec that has passed the Phase 6 shape gate.

Output: `ESTIMAND_TYPES` registered in `dsx/spec.py`, one new `_VALIDITY_FRAME_MEMBERSHIP` row, and
nine specs carrying the new field with no gate-result change anywhere in the corpus.
</objective>
