# BLOCKING CONSTRAINTS — Read Before Anything Else

> Each constraint below was discovered through an actual verification failure this
> session — an independent primary-source read of the loop's own citation pack.
> These are not predictions. Acknowledge each before writing any Phase 22 artifact.

- [ ] **CONSTRAINT: The perceptual ordering in the original pack was factually
  wrong.** Cleveland & McGill (1984) publish **6 ranks over 10 tasks WITH TIES**
  (p.536), not a 7-item strict order. Rank 3 is *"Length, direction, angle"*
  together, and p.537 states *"there is not enough information to separate the
  ties."* Heer & Bostock (p.206) independently: *"Theory also suggests that angle
  should perform worse than length, but the results do not support this."*
  → **Mitigation: REQ-P22-05's test asserts `rank(a) <= rank(b)`. A strict
  `length > angle` assertion must never ship — it has no support in either
  cited paper.**

- [ ] **CONSTRAINT: Two of the four proposed uncertainty chart names do not exist
  in Wilke.** "fan chart" appears nowhere in the book (verified against the
  full-text search index covering all 34 pages); "gradient CI band" is not his
  term — he has two *distinct* marks, "confidence strips" (continuous fade) and
  "graded confidence band" (nested levels). "half-eye" is real but lives in §5.6.
  → **Mitigation: REQ-P22-02 adopts Wilke's actual 10 marks from §5.6. Every
  vocabulary id must resolve to a real locator in the source.**

- [ ] **CONSTRAINT: The "FT Visual Vocabulary is MIT-licensed" claim is wrong.**
  The repo is MIT; the FT carves out its content in writing, twice — *"does not
  cover any FT content … all rights reserved."*
  → **Mitigation: attribute the nine-category axis, write our own descriptions,
  never vendor the PDF or copy its per-chart blurbs. Drop any "exhaustive" claim
  resting on the FT — the poster disclaims exhaustiveness itself.**

**Do not proceed until all boxes are checked.**

## Critical Anti-Patterns

| Pattern | Description | Severity | Prevention Mechanism |
|---------|-------------|----------|---------------------|
| Citation drift from teaching material | The pack's 7-item perceptual ordering traced to a Heer lecture slide + Mackinlay 1986, not to the paper it cited. It matched neither cleanly. | blocking | Every ranking/vocabulary shipped must be traced to the primary text and quoted at its locator, not to a secondary restatement — even a reputable one. |
| Inventing vocabulary names not in the source | "fan chart" and "gradient CI band" were plausible-sounding but absent from the cited book. | blocking | Each vocabulary id must carry a locator (chapter/figure) verified against the source before it ships. |
| Repo licence ≠ content licence | An MIT LICENSE file was read as covering all-rights-reserved publisher content sitting in the same repo. | blocking | For any reused source, read the README/content notices, not just the LICENSE file. |
| Citation attributing more than the source says | Few is real on gauges but never says "arbitrary maximum"; Munzner's anti-3D is justification-gated, not absolute. | advisory | State plainly which half of a rationale is the source's and which is DSX's own reasoning. |
| Citing a source that has been walked back | Datawrapper publicly reversed its dual-axis position in July 2026 while DSX bans it unconditionally. | advisory | Re-check live web sources for editorial amendments before relying on them for a hard gate. |
