# One-click checkout readout

**Audience:** VP Commerce
**Decision:** roll out one-click checkout if the completion-rate uplift clears the +1.0pp floor.

## Headline

The one-click checkout raises cart completion by 2.2 percentage points
(95% CI 1.4 to 3.0pp) among non-bot carts in the test window.

The interval clears the pre-declared +1.0pp practical floor, so the decision rule
recommends rollout. No guardrail degraded beyond its declared tolerance over the
two-week window.

## Limitations

This estimates the effect for active shoppers only and says nothing about dormant
users. It was measured over two weeks; seasonal cohorts may respond differently.
