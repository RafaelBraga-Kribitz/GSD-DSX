# Integrations gallery readout

**Audience:** Head of Platform
**Decision:** roll out the gallery if the installs-per-account uplift clears the +0.05 floor.

## Headline

The integrations gallery raises integrations installed by 0.12 per active
account (95% CI 0.07 to 0.17) in the test window.

The interval clears the pre-declared +0.05 practical floor, so the decision rule
recommends rollout. No guardrail degraded over the two-week window.

## Limitations

This counts installs, not sustained integration usage. It was measured over two
weeks; adoption is seasonal.
