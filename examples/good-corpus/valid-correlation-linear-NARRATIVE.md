# Linear-correlation readout (clean negative control)

**Audience:** VP Merchandising
**Status:** calibration control — a correctly-declared correlation that the gate
should pass silently.

## Headline

The bundle recommender raises average order value by $1.85
(95% CI $0.92 to $2.78) per completed order in the test window.

The numbers above are illustrative and pinned. This spec exists as a clean
negative control: it declares a Pearson correlation for a linear-association
estimand on a continuous (interval-or-better) operand — the coefficient's proper
home — so the correlation-routing gates reach their branches and stay silent.

## Limitations

A calibration control. It carries no decision weight and reports no real finding
about any product.
