# Share-nudge readout

**Audience:** Head of Growth
**Decision:** roll out the share-nudge if the referrals-per-user uplift clears the +0.05 floor.

## Headline

The share-nudge raises referrals sent by 0.14 per active user
(95% CI 0.08 to 0.20) in the test window.

The interval clears the pre-declared +0.05 practical floor, so the decision rule
recommends rollout. No guardrail degraded over the two-week window.

## Limitations

This counts invitations sent, not referrals accepted, and says nothing about
downstream conversion. It was measured over two weeks; sharing behaviour is seasonal.
