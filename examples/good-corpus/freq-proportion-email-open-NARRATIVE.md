# Subject-line rewrite readout

**Audience:** Lifecycle Marketing Lead
**Decision:** adopt the rewritten subject lines if the open-rate uplift clears the +1.0pp floor.

## Headline

The subject-line rewrite raises email open rate by 2.1 percentage points
(95% CI 1.3 to 2.9pp) among delivered recipients in the test window.

The interval clears the pre-declared +1.0pp practical floor, so the decision rule
recommends adoption. No guardrail degraded over the two-week window.

## Limitations

This measures opens, not downstream clicks or conversions. It was measured over two
weeks; open behaviour varies by campaign calendar.
