# Distraction-free editor readout

**Audience:** Head of Product
**Decision:** ship the editor if the focused-minutes uplift clears the +0.5 minute floor.

## Headline

The distraction-free editor raises focused editing time by 1.4 minutes
(95% CI 0.8 to 2.0) per active session in the test window.

The interval clears the pre-declared +0.5 minute practical floor, so the decision
rule recommends shipping. No guardrail degraded over the two-week window.

## Limitations

This measures focused minutes, not output quality. It was measured over two weeks;
usage patterns are seasonal.
