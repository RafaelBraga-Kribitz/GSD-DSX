# 2026-Q2 churn-by-product-holding readout (Phase 28 measurement spike)

**Audience:** VP Retention
**Status:** descriptive readout — a quarter-over-segment churn summary. Measurement
input only; not a committed corpus fixture.

## Headline

Customers holding fewer than two products churn at 27% against 18% for the rest of
the book, over the 2026-Q2 window.

That gap is measured across the full book of 5,000 customers active at the start of
the quarter (n=5,000), split into the fewer-than-two-products segment and the rest.

## Supporting reads (other metrics)

Two supporting comparisons were computed on OTHER metrics over the same book:
revenue-per-user and activation-rate both show a small-to-moderate gap between the
fewer-than-two-products segment and the rest. Neither is a test of churn itself; the
churn figures above are descriptive shares, not tested effects.

## Limitations

A descriptive quarter-over-segment readout. It identifies no cause of the churn gap
and runs no statistical test on churn itself.
